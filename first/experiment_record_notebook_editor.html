SECTION 04 // PROTOCOL RUN Step-by-Step Observations
BENCH TIMER: 01h 12m ELAPSED
Kinetic Mathematical Model Michaelis-Menten Fit
v₀ = (V_max · [S]) / (K_m + [S])
Where [S] = target DNA duplex (nM), V_max = 1.42 ± 0.04 nM·s⁻¹, K_m = 18.3 nM

Reaction initiated strictly at 37.0 °C using calibrated Eppendorf ThermoMixer C (Equip-ID: #EQ-7721). Cleavage kinetics monitored at predetermined intervals of 5, 10, 15, 30, and 60 minutes.

At each timepoint, a 5.0 µL aliquot was withdrawn with positive displacement micropipette and immediately quenched with 1.0 µL of 6X Stop Solution (EDTA 250mM), followed by rapid heat-inactivation at 65 °C for 10 min. No precipitation or visible aggregation observed upon stopping.
